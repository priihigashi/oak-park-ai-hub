# Reels: visual, storytelling, sound, endings, safe zones (her rules)

## Visual / layout
- Never leave a colored phrase alone on its own line. Every text block fills the line before wrapping; check ALL line breaks. Text in balloons/boxes is centered in the shape. Audit = screenshot the final card of each scene and fix alignment/breaks.
- Text appears early enough to read; nothing new in the last ~1.5-2 s before a cut. Too much text = cut it. No fully static screen for more than ~3 s; reveal each text at the START of the sentence that mentions it.
- Animations match the narration word by word (a list in the voice = one animated icon per item on its word). Plan beat by beat before building. Any chance for an animation or chart: do it unasked. Chart bars grow in one after another.
- Big numbers, sometimes one word much bigger; modern + minimal; few text boxes; alternate font weights; consistent look.
- Person cutouts: real photo head (never initials), big, thick white outline, slight wobble; no head when their own clip is on screen. Covers: half-body cutouts.
- People without a party (economists, commentators): small positioning chip ("liberal, centro-direita"). Never anyone's @ in reels.
- Fine print never near/below the caption. Collage kit sizes: fine print 23px, notes 24px, source 22px, sublabels 20px. Spread content full width and down to the caption; no gap above caption.
- Phrases starting a block start with a capital letter.
- Previews with speaker clips: real clips play in the preview. Previews built from someone else's video: tab with the full original video.
- "Toque pra continuar assistindo" banner from 0:50 to 1:00 (+ "vendo pelo story? toque aqui"), below the caption and above the handle, light pulse, silent.
- @tentandochegaratevoce handle: plain text, no pill, very bottom (black on light, white on dark).
- Covers: grander than inside slides, readable in the profile grid; topic huge + main call big in the middle; deadline if any; overlays; don't repeat the last post's background color. Covers spark curiosity for BOTH sides, never give away the verdict. Made as soon as v1 of the video exists.
- Part 2 videos look different (color, photos, cover).
- AI images: no glitches (fake money, garbled text), people look Brazilian, shown with dignity; free/public images by default; temporary image with label if not found; paid AI (Replicate) only after her ok.
- "Telas de descanso": rest screens with a giant edge-to-edge title + good image; giant poster type, vazado, light-yellow serif with sparkles.
- Animated carousels: card 1 has everything on screen from frame 0 (it's the cover); ambient motion only.
- When a reel runs long: don't remove on your own; scene notes in the preview with ⭐ keep / ◐ optional / ✕ would cut.
- Credit nobody (creators, outlets) without asking her first.
- Ask before lowering quality/fluidity to hit a deadline.
- Cool vibe (coolonização): B&W photos with paper texture, duotone (orange/mustard + black), B&W with one element in color, half-body people, smooth motion, faded blacks, color as differentiator; palette options switchable in previews (orange, mustard, autumn). New templates go to the coolonização reference preview alongside the others, for every page.
- One skill per visual style. Style references saved in Drive News > Templates > Estilos e Referências + GitHub.
- News palette: no beige/cream backgrounds; Brazilian "raiz" colors; diverse, warm, smiling people.
- Foreign-language clips: PT translation caption right under the speakers.
- Clip cards: rounded corners + thin black outline slightly off and trembling; hide other outlets' logos; caption font less heavy.

## Storytelling
- Retention: hook in first ~3 s matching the cover; by ~10 s say what the viewer gets and start delivering; every ~15 s a reason to stay; partial reward mid-video; overall reward before the end.
- Opening promise is clear, not cryptic ("por que isso acontece, quem tá por trás e o que a gente pode fazer").
- No false balance; give gravity (chance of going wrong, how irreversible). If no balance can be determined, say so.
- From the FIRST reference video: create the topic folder, save its transcription (all videos in one doc, appended), add to tracker; keep a running feedback list and reread it each version.
- Our reel should be the best of all references; under ~3 min target; careful at 3:30-4 min; avoid 5 min.
- Every narrated sentence has an image/animation. Mix styles (2D first; 3D only after the story is good, at the most engaging moments). Talking-head moments feel natural.
- Explain a mechanism fully ONCE at the step where the doubt arises, then go fast, end with before/after recap.
- Content should reach both sides; hardest audience = fearful right. Say the fear out loud first ("tem gente achando que...") then correct. Suggestions in optional tone ("você poderia pedir..."); attribute suggestions to third parties ("tem gente que sugere").
- Never leave vague pointers ("isso", "daí") loose.
- Objective narration: no process narration, short CTAs, no speeding the voice to fit; no backstage ("eu cortei o vídeo...").
- Use her dictated wording lightly polished, keep her tics ("a gente verificou", "até o momento", "mas, sim", "e é isso que eu vou te mostrar"); avoid "tá rodando", "não é bem assim"; never use the defending side's praise words.
- Layperson read separate from fact audit ("pé e cabeça"; explain term before using it; charts stay on screen long enough).
- "Tá meio estranho / robotizado / sem flow" = push further toward her conversational way.
- Compare reference videos line by line for lines that explain better; improve ours in our own words.
- Say "ele" right after showing the person; repeat the name when saying something negative.
- Make connections in the viewer's brain: remind them of the earlier fact a later one links to.

## Sound
- Every reel comes with low background music fitting the topic's culture (offer options), and SFX on key animations. Rotate music between videos. UGC calm music: lo-fi and marimba. No "tum tum tum" beats under news.
- Narration as ONE continuous take, then split (no robotic sentence endings). Pauses always have narration or music.
- Free draft voice first; paid voice only after full approval and after confirming which voice ("o Senhor", Senhor M.); send her the exact text before any paid re-record. Gentle voice cleanup only. Check balance/cost first.
- Dubbed reels: voice gender matches the person; keep background ambience; keep burned-in subtitles.

## Endings (CTA)
- News CTA: "Pra ajudar a aumentar o engajamento, copia o link, curte, reposta aqui e nos stories, e segue a página pra não perder o próximo." Follow ask appears only once. Close with "e já segue pra mais conteúdo assim" only if not already asked.
- Civic-action topics: say viewers can help; comments get every way to participate (e-mails, ready letter, links to vote on bills/consultas); invite them to write their own version. Prefer left-wing authors' bills.
- The audit (folder, ROTEIRO, ChatGPT audit prompt, review) runs automatically whenever a reel skill starts.

## Safe zones (1080x1920, Reels + Stories)
- Text zone: x 150-915, y 268-1680. Nothing above y 268 (Stories header). Main text ends ~y 1500. Handle y 1626, source y 1668. Nothing below y 1840.
- Reels viewer zooms ~1.3x (cuts ~52 px each side, ~105 px top); right buttons cover x > ~930 from y ~1060 down.

- Background can bleed; main elements never near the right edge.
