# Brand Voice

**Trigger:** "apply brand voice / style pass / humanize / make this sound like me / run through brand-voice" — on any draft copy.

## Niche Definitions

| Niche | Scope |
|-------|-------|
| `marketing` | OPC project posts, McFolling property/rental content, ads copy, captions |
| `personal` | Priscila's personal IG/TikTok, personal essays, reflections |
| `editorial` | Opinion pieces, thought-leadership, commentary (OPC or personal) |
| `news` | Factual news recaps, summaries, curated headlines (Brazil + US niches) |
| `fact_check` | Verificamos, Verdade Pela Metade, and FORMAT-013/024 debunk content |

---

## Style Passes — What Each One Does

### Pass A1 — Break Uniformity (rewritten)
Rewrites for sentence variety: break repetitive structures, vary length, fix monotony.
- **all niches:** apply fully
- **news + fact_check:** style-only — adjust sentence rhythm; do NOT reframe the assertion, soften certainty, or change who is being quoted

### Pass A2 — Skeptical Reader
Read as a skeptical audience member. Remove hype, unearned claims, and hollow filler.
- **all niches:** apply fully

### Pass A3 — Natural Rhythm
Make the copy sound spoken aloud — cut stiff phrasing, add natural contractions, loosen formal constructions.
- **all niches:** apply fully
- **news + fact_check:** guarded — do not add colloquialisms that could dilute factual precision; contractions OK, idioms off

### Pass B4 — Point of View
Inject voice and perspective: "we believe," "this is why we care," a personal angle.
- **marketing:** YES
- **personal:** YES
- **editorial:** YES
- **news:** NO — voice must stay neutral; do not editorialize reported facts
- **fact_check:** NO — do not introduce perspective into a claim verification

### Pass B5 — Lived Language (rewritten)
Replace generic language with specific, grounded, sensory, real-world phrasing. Rewrite from scratch if needed.
- **all niches:** apply fully

### Pass C6 — Memorable Cut
Trim to the most striking version: cut weak endings, frontload the hook, cut the last sentence if it limps.
- **all niches:** apply fully
- **news + fact_check:** guarded — cut for length and punch, but never cut the caveat, the source attribution, or the uncertainty qualifier

## Non-Negotiable Rule (verbatim — applies to ALL niches and ALL passes)

> A style pass may change language and structure; it may not silently change the proposition being asserted, the evidence supporting it, who observed it, or how certain the source is.

---

## Hard Limit — OPC + McFolling Marketing

**Never generate or humanize a customer testimonial.**

FTC 16 CFR Part 465 (effective Oct 21 2024) bans AI-generated fake reviews and testimonials — up to **$51,744 per violation**. This applies whether the testimonial is invented or "based on" a real one that is rewritten to sound more compelling. If Priscila supplies a real customer quote, transcribe it; do not embellish it.

## Workflow

1. **Identify niche** from context (ask if unclear — do not guess).
2. **Identify passes** to run: apply all passes marked ✅ or ⚠️ for that niche; skip ❌ passes.
3. **Run passes in order**: A1 → A2 → A3 → B4 → B5 → C6.
4. **Apply each ⚠️ constraint** literally — flag any place where the constraint blocked a change.
5. **Output**: revised copy. For news/fact_check, also output a one-line note listing what was left unchanged and why.

## Done check
- Niche declared at top of output
- All applicable passes noted
- Factual record, source attribution, certainty qualifiers untouched
- No invented testimonials or fake social proof
- For news/fact_check: constraint log included
