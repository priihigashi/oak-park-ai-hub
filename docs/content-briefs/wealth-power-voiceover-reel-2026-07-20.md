# Wealth/Power Carousel — Voiceover Reel Script (EN)
Daily Advancer · 2026-07-20

Format: FORMAT-021 dual-output. 60–90 seconds. ElevenLabs narration synced to slide PNGs.
Estimated read time at 130 wpm: ~75 seconds (168 words).
Tone: measured, clear, direct — not angry, not preachy. Factual and personal.

---

## REEL SCRIPT (EN — final narration pass)

---

They didn't win a fair race. They inherited the finish line.

Go back far enough and the richest people were almost never regular workers. They owned the thing everyone else needed — the land, the ships, the factories, the mines. When you own that, money flows to you while other people do the work.

By 1860, the roughly four million enslaved people in the United States were the single largest financial asset in the entire American economy — worth more than all factories and railroads combined. That wealth didn't vanish after the Civil War. It got passed down.

Between now and 2048, $124 trillion in wealth will pass from one generation to the next in the US alone. The households that make up just two percent of the population account for more than half of it.

And then there's the tax trick. The 25 richest Americans grew $401 billion richer in four years and paid an effective rate of 3.4%. A teacher can't do that with a paycheck.

The problem isn't the person. It's the amount of power. And it still sets your rent, your wages, and your future.

## PRODUCTION NOTES

- Use ElevenLabs voice: a calm, clear American-English male or female narrator (not dramatic, not newscaster — think documentary, measured).
- Sync narration to slide PNGs in order: slide 1 (cover visual) → slide 2 → slide 5 (slavery stat) → slide 7 ($124T) → slide 10 (3.4%) → slide 15 (close).
- Do NOT try to narrate all 20 slides — select the anchor moments above and let the visuals carry the rest.
- Target: 60–75 seconds. If ElevenLabs runs long on a passage, tighten "went passed down" → "was passed down"; "between now and 2048" → "over the next 25 years."
- Reel should stand alone (no carousel needed to understand it) — it is the hook that drives people to swipe through the full carousel.
- Caption for the reel: use the Part 1 caption above with "Watch + swipe 👆" added at the end.

## PT REEL SCRIPT (for Brazil-niche feed — same structure, translated)

---

Eles não venceram uma corrida justa. Eles herdaram a linha de chegada.

Volte o suficiente no tempo e os mais ricos quase nunca eram trabalhadores comuns. Eles eram donos daquilo que todo mundo precisava — a terra, os navios, as fábricas, as minas. Quando você é dono disso, o dinheiro flui pra você enquanto os outros fazem o trabalho.

Em 1860, os cerca de quatro milhões de escravizados nos Estados Unidos eram o maior ativo financeiro de toda a economia americana — valendo mais do que todas as fábricas e ferrovias somadas. Essa riqueza não evaporou depois da Guerra Civil. Foi passada adiante.

De agora até 2048, US$124 trilhões em riqueza vão trocar de mãos só nos EUA. As famílias que representam apenas dois por cento da população concentram mais da metade disso.

E tem o truque fiscal. Os vinte e cinco americanos mais ricos ficaram US$401 bilhões mais ricos em quatro anos e pagaram uma taxa efetiva de 3,4%. Um professor não faz isso com o salário.

O problema não é a pessoa. É a quantidade de poder. E isso ainda define o seu aluguel, o seu salário e o seu futuro.
