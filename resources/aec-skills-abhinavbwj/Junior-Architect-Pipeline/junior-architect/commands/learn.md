description: The junior's learning record. Predictions against results, what the checker caught, what the junior caught, and which tasks to practise at lower autonomy.
---

Delegate to the **mentor** agent.

1. Run the pipeline CLI's `log` and read `.jap/learning_log.csv`, plus the `prediction.md`, `report.json` and `review.md` of each closed run in `.jap/runs/`.
2. Per task type, show:
   - number of runs;
   - prediction against result (for area schedules, the predicted total against the checked total);
   - checker errors;
   - junior findings, meaning the items the checker missed and the junior caught.
3. Recommend, per task:
   - repeat at A0 or A1 where predictions are consistently off, or where the junior catches nothing beyond the checker;
   - run at the ceiling where predictions are close and the junior adds findings.
4. Name one concept to study before the next run, with its primary source: the code clause, the area standard, ISO 19650, or Schön on reflection-in-action.
